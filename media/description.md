## Tagline

Ride the lift to the top of High Peaks, a cartoon ski resort where a very relaxed yeti runs the mountain. A 7x5 cluster slot with tumbling wins, Ice Bongs that turn a whole column into multipliers, four fixed jackpots and three bonus rounds. Max win 50,000x.

## Description

Fresh snow, blue sky and a yeti who has clearly been here a while. High Peaks is a 7x5 cluster game set on a cartoon mountain summit: land 5 or more matching symbols joined up, down, left or right and they pay, clear and tumble, with new symbols dropping in for as long as the wins keep coming.

ICE BONG
When an Ice Bong lands it drops down its whole column, rips, and leaves a multiplier on every cell from top to bottom: x2 up to x64, or MAX. Any cluster touching a MAX column pays the max win.

JACKPOTS
A ? can drop onto the board on any spin. When the spin has played out, the jackpot board spins and stops on a fixed prize: MINI 10x, MINOR 50x, MAJOR 250x or GRAND 2,500x the bet.

BONUS ROUNDS
- STASH: 3 PEAK Tokens. Sticky Buds worth x0.2 to x500 pile up while the spin gauge lasts, and every new token adds 5 spins.
- FRESH POWDER: 4 or more PEAK Tokens. 10 free spins where every cell multiplier stays for the whole bonus and keeps growing.
- BLACK DIAMOND: 3 Black Diamond Tokens. The night run and the biggest bonus on the mountain, with Ice Bongs landing far more often.
In FRESH POWDER and BLACK DIAMOND, 3 or more PEAK Tokens at the end of a free spin add 5 more.

BONUS BUY AND SPECIAL SPINS
Buy STASH, FRESH POWDER or BLACK DIAMOND directly, or switch on HIGHER CHANCE or AVALANCHE SPIN for a different kind of spin. The total cost is always shown before you confirm.
