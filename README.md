# High Peaks

A cartoon ski-resort slot for Stake Engine, built on the Falcos engine and math: a 7x5 board with cluster pays and
tumbles, cell multipliers, RTP 96.50% in every mode and a max win of 50,000x. New in High Peaks: **MINI / MINOR /
MAJOR / GRAND jackpots** (10x / 50x / 250x / 2,500x the bet), won when a **?** drops onto the board.

**From [zeqpz/poweroutage](https://github.com/zeqpz/poweroutage), unchanged (owner, 2026-11-19):** the
black-and-white Smukiez intro (it holds while the game loads, its thin white bar filling), the three intro boxes
(the game's title over three cream feature cards - here the jackpot **?**, the FS scatter and the XFS scatter -
dealt out from a stack, CLICK TO PLAY under them) and the HUD (its navy bar, fixed BALANCE | WIN | BET columns and
`frontend/hudfit.js`, which shrinks a long figure to its slot and keeps the bonus emblem off the bar).
`tools/highpeaks/port_po_ui.py <poweroutage/frontend>` made the port; it is exact-string edits, so it documents
what came over. The Bonus Buy menu layout is still the base game's.

Everything else was made new: the scene and its animation (`frontend/skyfx.js`: chairs riding the lift, flat
cartoon skiers and snowboarders on the run, chimney smoke, drifting clouds, falling snow, flickering windows), the
log board frame and plank backing, the framed jackpot board, the treasure-chest Buy Bonus emblem, symbols, banners,
logos, gauges and all five music cues (Lyria via OpenRouter, `tools/music`: reggae-tinged chill house, in keys that
match the sound effects).

No 3D-looking characters (owner, 2026-11-19): a Blender-rendered duck stood by the board and skied the slope for a
day; it did not fit the flat cartoon and `tools/highpeaks/remove_duck.py` took it out (git history keeps its pipeline).

**The peak pass (owner, 2026-11-19/20):**
- The scene is the TOP of a mountain (`bonus_wall.webp` and the three bonus plates: fresh powder, black diamond night,
  avalanche), drawn by `art/highpeaks/jobs/peak_jobs.py` + `tools/highpeaks/orgen.py` and installed by
  `tools/highpeaks/peak_install.py`. `frontend/skyfx.js` animates it: clouds, the hut's chimney smoke and windows,
  gusts of spindrift, falling snow - no skiers or riders (owner: "get rid of the people going down the slope").
- The board keeps its log outline, redrawn with a sign board on the top log; `assets/logo.webp` sits on it
  (`#framelogo`, the sign's face in `frame_*.json` -> `--sg-*`).
- Win art in the logo's style: snow-capped ice titles (BIG / MEGA / EPIC / TOTAL WIN), a snow-burst splash, wooden
  amount plates, the numerals' drop shadow icy blue.
- No graffiti type: every Falcos street face (CLICK TO CONTINUE among them) is Fredoka now.
- The Bonus Buy panel is black at half opacity with white keylines and type.
- FS / XFS glow at rest (`boardgl.js` scatIdle: turning rays, a halo in their colour, breathing, a passing flash);
  once two are down, each reel still to land is a lit gold column while it drops slower.
- The HUD's balance no longer gets cut off: its change pop stays inside its column (`hudfit.js` keeps room for it).
- **The yeti**: an original cartoon yeti in ski gear with a joint, his snowboard in the snow beside him - a flat 2D
  cut-out rig made and animated in Blender (`tools/highpeaks/yeti/`), played live by `frontend/charrig.js` +
  `frontend/yeti.js` (test page `frontend/yeti_test.html`). He stands right of the board on a landscape screen and
  reacts to the round (`charReact`: anticipation, win, big_win, bonus, lose).

**The animation pass (owner, 2026-11-23: "icon animations when they win, idle, drop and disappear need to be better ...
use a plugin or tool to rig"):**
- Every symbol is a real 2D rig now, not a picture scaled up and down: `tools/highpeaks/blender/symrig.py` puts each
  `art/highpeaks/symbols_static/<ID>.png` on a mesh cut to its alpha, gives it a skeleton (a spine plus a chain for every
  loose part: pom-pom, steam, cheese drips, flame, hanger...), skins it with Blender's automatic weights and lets the
  **Jiggle Physics** add-on (extensions.blender.org, `jiggle_physics` 4.5.3) simulate the loose parts, baked to keys.
  Four clips each: `land` (the settle, with two puffs of snow), `idle` (a breath, a sway, a glint), `hit` (a win move of
  its own per symbol: the coin flips, the pizza swings on its crust, the lighter flares, the cannon fires...) and `burn`
  (a flash, then the picture breaks into shards with the item's own debris). All 18 render headless in about 90 s:
  `blender -b --python tools/highpeaks/blender/symrig.py` (no `--factory-startup`), look at them with
  `tools/highpeaks/blender/symqa.py <dir>` (contact sheets, and a check that nothing touches its clip's box), then
  `blender_pack.py`, `align_clips.py`, `centre_symbols.py` as before. The sheets hold about a quarter more pixels than
  the static ones did (the win and the exit have a 13-unit box).
- The yeti's skeleton is generated by **Rigify** now (`tools/highpeaks/yeti/yeti_rigify.py`: a metarig from the old
  joint positions, IK/FK limbs with two deform bones a section, 47 exported bones for 25), his torso, legs and board arm
  skinned by automatic weights, and 22 secondary controls (pom-pom, beanie, goggles, cheeks, beard, hood, belly, hems,
  cuffs...) simulated by Jiggle Physics and baked. All six clips were re-authored on the control rig: a real crouch and
  star jump in `big_win`, the board ripped out of the snow in `bonus`, a body that drops link by link in `lose`.
  `yeti_pack.py`, `charrig.js` and `yeti.js` are unchanged; `qa_rig.py` / `qa_runtime.py` / `qa_sheet.py` check it
  (planted boots, loop seams, the joint on his lips, the real runtime in headless Chrome). The old `yeti_rig.py` stays
  for reference.
- **The scene in layers** (owner, 2026-11-23: "redo the background ... a foreground, midground and background"; then
  "dont make the background and foreground move"): new art from `art/highpeaks/jobs/layers_jobs.py` (far peaks and sky;
  the summit, drawn on magenta and keyed by `tools/highpeaks/layers_install.py` because the model gives 4K only opaque;
  a pine, the flag's cloth, a foreground drift and a pine bough), laid over the plate by `frontend/layers.js` from
  `assets/scene/layers/layers.json`. NOTHING SLIDES: the layers lie still on each other and the corner pieces sit in the
  plate's own camera layer. What moves is in the picture: clouds crossing behind the summit, chairs riding the lift
  cable, the flag, the pines' sway, glints on the snow (`art/highpeaks/jobs/layers_places.json` says where each stands).
  `assets/bonus_wall.webp` is the same picture flattened (first paint, loading screen, reduced motion). The three bonus
  scenes are still single plates laid over it.
- **The Ice Bong** is the column-multiplier symbol (GP; it was the Snow Cannon and its dial). One cartoon drawing in SVG,
  `frontend/icebong.js`: `svg()` is the icon (`tools/highpeaks/icebong.py` photographs it into `symbols_static/GP.png`
  for `symrig.py`), `tall()` is the show - a see-through tube over the whole column on a round base (owner, with a
  picture: "it should just look like this shape"). `pumpShow`: it drops down the column and every icon it breaks goes as
  its foot passes ("all the icons break as the bong falls down in one animation"), then it is ripped - the heap on its
  bowl burns, smoke climbs the tube - and the multiplier slams onto the middle of it. `icebong_test.html` shows both.
- **H1 is a pair of skis** (owner: "make the normal bong icon skis"; `art/highpeaks/raw/sym_H1_skis.png`).
- **Bonus Buy**: five cards, each its own scene with the game's yeti (`art/highpeaks/jobs/extras_jobs.py`, the yeti's
  master picture as the reference), and a new bonus button (the yeti bursting out of the chest in a ring of ice).
- **Win numbers cut from ice**: `tools/highpeaks/ice_numerals.py` redraws `assets/num/*` as faceted blocks with a cap of
  snow (the white ones are kept in `art/highpeaks/ui_flat/num/`); the board's win pop-ups (`.winpop`) take the same look.
- `tools/highpeaks/qa_shots.py` photographs moments of the real game in headless Chrome (scene, menu, win, the bong
  firing, the `alert` strip, a `cam` move, or `replay<book id>` for a real round) - the way to look at an animation
  mid-flight.
- **2026-11-23, later**: the ice bong's show is drawn for its column (`IceBong.tall(vh)`: rim over the top row, foot on
  the bottom one) and carries no text at all (`BONG_TEXT` in index.html: no grade names, no figure on the tube - the cells and the line
  under the board say the multiplier; no DUSTING / POWDER / BLIZZARD names in the ticker or the
  rules); its bowl burns a real flame (`tools/highpeaks/flame_sheet.py` -> `assets/fx/flame.webp`, 12 looping frames)
  over real smoke (`smoke_tile.py`). The yeti's box lives in `#camfar` now (`placeYeti`): he pans, zooms and rolls
  with the scene on a camera move and stands under the foreground pieces (`.lyfront`, z 3 over his 1). The alert
  strip (`.notice`: RAPID ON / OFF, errors) is the HUD's fill, outline and face, one thin line as wide as the board,
  placed under its bottom log. The spin's background hop (`bgbounce`) is gone; the board keeps its pop.
- The yeti stands IN the scene: his outlined mound is left out (`charrig.js` `hide`), a drift of the scene's snow is
  drawn round his boots and the foot of his board (`ground`, `assets/yeti/drift.webp`), and he and it take the light of
  whichever scene is up (`tint`; `Layers.mood`, read off each plate's snow by the installer) as `Scene.to` changes it.
  The scene's dim is lighter (.12 - .3, was the night station's .2 - .42).
- The yeti's canvas and atlas are sized by the pixels he is really shown at (`charrig.js`: a phone used to draw him on
  a 2316 x 1916 antialiased buffer from a 4096 x 2048 atlas), he casts a soft shadow on the snow and stands on the
  frame's bottom edge, and the green chimney smoke is off (`skyfx.js` SMOKE): behind the jackpot board it read as a
  little tree growing on GRAND.

Lobby art for engine.io, built from a BACKGROUND and a FOREGROUND the way the studio's Media Editor builds a tile

## Layout

| Part | Where | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front end | `frontend/` | visuals, UI, animation, sound. No outcome math. |
| Math | `math-sdk/games/highpeaks/` | config, reel strips, sims, optimisation, published books (`library/publish_files`). |
| Local RGS | `rgs/mock_rgs.py` | a stand-in for the Stake Engine RGS, for testing only. |
| Art and sound tools | `tools/highpeaks/`, `art/highpeaks/` | OpenRouter image generation (`orgen.py`, prompts in `art/highpeaks/jobs/`), `install_art.py`, `static_symbols.py`, the jackpot sounds (`sfx/jackpot.py`). |

The OpenRouter key is read from `~/.highpeaks/openrouter-key.txt`. It is never stored in the repo.

## Play it locally

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install pillow zstandard numpy scipy soundfile pyloudnorm imageio-ffmpeg noisereduce
.venv/bin/python rgs/mock_rgs.py
```

then open http://localhost:8755/index.html?sessionID=test&rgs_url=http://localhost:8755&lang=en&device=desktop

Watch a jackpot round (base-game book 1005 drops a ? and spins to MAJOR):
http://localhost:8755/index.html?replay=true&game=highpeaks&version=1&mode=base&event=1005&amount=1000000&rgs_url=http://localhost:8755&lang=en&device=desktop

## Rebuild the math

```bash
cd math-sdk/games/highpeaks
../../.venv/bin/python run.py --modes fillup premium nitro midnight --others 20000 --threads 8 --batch 1250 --optimize
../../.venv/bin/python run.py --modes base --base 100000 --threads 8 --optimize
../../.venv/bin/python run.py --modes chance --others 50000 --threads 8 --optimize
../../.venv/bin/python run.py --no-sims --checks
../../.venv/bin/python verify_books.py --write-index
../../.venv/bin/python jackpot_stats.py
```
